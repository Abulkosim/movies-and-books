## Cross-cutting threads to notice

A few ideas keep echoing across the list. Watching them lets you see the books as a network rather than a stack.

- **Attention as a finite resource** — Newport, Hari, Lanier, Wallace, Waitzkin, Seneca, the Stoics. The fight for your own focus is older than smartphones.
- **Suffering as the path to meaning** — Frankl, Dostoevsky, Camus, Kalanithi, Hesse, the Quran/Islamic readings, Tolstoy late period. Different traditions, same conclusion.
- **Power and how it actually moves** — Machiavelli, Greene, Chomsky, Orwell, Huxley, Taleb, the Dostoevsky "Grand Inquisitor". The honest map vs. the polite one.
- **Compounding** — Clear, Naval, Buffett-via-Naval, Aitmatov's slow time, Tolstoy's late conversion. Small inputs, long horizons, non-linear outputs.
- **The lonely man vs. society** — Camus, Kafka, Dostoevsky's Underground Man, Hesse, Chekhov's "Man in a Case", Wilde's Dorian. Different costumes, same wound.
- **Engineering as a humanities discipline** — Ousterhout, Graham, Feynman, Deutsch. Code is thought; thought has aesthetics; aesthetics matter.
