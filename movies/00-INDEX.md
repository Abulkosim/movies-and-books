## How to read these

Each entry has the same structure:
- **What it's really about** — the theme under the plot
- **Key scenes** — moments that carry the load
- **Easy to miss** — a layer most viewers walk past
- **The test** — one question; if you can answer it, you got the film
- **Connections** — links to other titles in this collection

Films that appear in multiple themes (e.g., *Black Swan* — madness *and* horror) live in their best-fit file and get pointers from elsewhere.

## Coverage

All ~210 unique titles from `movies.md` are filed somewhere. Duplicates (Stranger Things ×3, Home Alone ×3, Interstellar ×2, Aftersun ×2, Dark ×2, Before Sunrise / Before Trilogy, Three Idiots, Godfather, Departed) are treated as one entry. A handful of shorts and music-video pieces (*Sleep on the Floor*, *A True Gentleman*) get briefer entries because the form is brief.
